The .mp3 files in this directory are Visual Studio Code's accessibility signals.
They are not original to Earshot. They come from the microsoft/vscode repository,
at src/vs/platform/accessibilitySignal/browser/media, and are redistributed here
under that repository's MIT licence, reproduced in full below.

They are used rather than replaced on purpose: they were designed for people who
listen to their editor rather than look at it, so the events that matter most are
already distinct from each other by intent.
